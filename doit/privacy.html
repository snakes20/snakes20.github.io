Privacy Policy — My Do-It List

Last updated: August 14, 2026

My Do-It List ("the App") is a to-do list app developed by Jake B. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

The App does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not collect or transmit your tasks, lists, or any personal content to us or to any server. All task data is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (SQLite) and is not accessible to us.

Advertising

The App displays ads using Google AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads). Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can review or control ad personalization through your device's ad settings, and you can learn more about how Google handles this data at Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal data. We do not have access to any data collected by third-party advertising services beyond aggregate, non-identifying analytics that Google may make available to us.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Since your task data never leaves your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting your data within the App or by uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
