Delete Your Can It! Account

How to request deletion of your account and cloud-synced data.

Steps to delete your account

1

Email jakedev82@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my Can It account".

2

Include the email address you used to sign in to CanIt Cloud, so we can find and remove your account.

3

We'll confirm by email once your account and synced data have been deleted — typically within 7 days.

Request Account Deletion

What gets deleted

  • Your account (sign-in email and authentication record)
  • All cloud-synced data tied to your account: recipes, ingredients, instructions, canning sessions, jars, and storage locations

What isn't affected

  • Data stored only on your device — Can It! works fully offline, and if you never enabled CanIt Cloud, your data never left your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Purchase/subscription records, which are managed by Apple, Google Play, and RevenueCat for entitlement purposes and aren't affected by this request. For refunds or purchase history, contact Apple or Google Play directly.
