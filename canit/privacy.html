Privacy Policy — Can It!

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Can It! ("the App") is a canning and food preservation tracking app, developed by Jake B, that helps you manage your jars and recipes. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

By default, the App does not require an account or sign-in. Your jars, recipes, and other content are stored locally on your device and are not collected or transmitted to us, unless you choose to enable Cloud Sync as described below.

Optional Cloud Sync (CanIt Cloud Subscription)

The App offers an optional "CanIt Cloud" subscription that unlocks additional features (such as unlimited jars/recipes and an ad-free experience) and, separately, the option to sync and back up your data across devices.

  • Subscribing is processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play billing (via RevenueCat) and does not by itself require you to create an account or give us any personal information — payment and billing details are handled entirely by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat, not by us.
  • Enabling sync is a separate, optional step available to subscribers. You sign in with a passwordless email code: you enter your email address, we send a 6-digit verification code to that email, and you enter the code to sign in. We never ask for or store a password. Once signed in, your jars, recipes, and related app data are synced and stored using our database provider, Supabase, so they're available across your devices.
  • You can also link an additional device using a "scan to link" QR code, without re-entering your email or a code.
  • If you subscribe but never enable sync, your subscription entitlements still work locally and no personal data is sent to us.

Advertising

The App displays ads using Google AdMob (react-native-google-mobile-ads) for users who have not subscribed to CanIt Cloud. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can review or control ad personalization through your device's ad settings, and you can learn more about how Google handles this data at Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites. Subscribing to CanIt Cloud removes ads.

Payment Processing

Subscription purchases are handled by RevenueCat and processed through Apple's App Store or Google Play billing systems. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell your personal data. Data associated with an optional CanIt Cloud sign-in (your email address and synced app data) is stored with our database provider, Supabase, solely to provide the sync/backup feature, and is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes. We do not have access to any data collected by third-party advertising services beyond aggregate, non-identifying analytics that Google may make available to us.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

If you never enable Cloud Sync, your data never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time by deleting your data within the App or by uninstalling the App. If you've enabled Cloud Sync, you can request deletion of your synced data and account at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
